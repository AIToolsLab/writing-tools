# Application Name

Thoughtful AI Tools

# Short Description

AI helps you think and reflect while writing, not write for you.

# Detailed Description

Thoughtful AI Tools helps you write thoughtfully by keeping you fully engaged with your own ideas. Instead of generating text for you, it helps you see your document — its structure, main points, concepts, and gaps — so you can decide what to work on next.

Open the sidebar in any document and use it to:

Visualize your document — generate hierarchical outlines, possible structures, and exemplar-based outlines to understand how your writing is organized.
Analyze your content — surface main points, key concepts, claims and arguments, potential counterarguments, and the questions a reader might ask.
Find where to work next — get a prioritized list of places in your draft that could use attention, each linked directly to the relevant text.
Jump across tabs — select a word or phrase and instantly find and navigate to where it appears in your document's other tabs.
Every suggestion links back to specific parts of your document, so you stay grounded in your own writing. Thoughtful AI Tools is designed to support thinking and reflection — you remain the author.
